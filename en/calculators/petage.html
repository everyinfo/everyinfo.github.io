🐾 Pet Age Calculator

Convert your dog or cat's age to human years, using veterinary reference charts.

What is a Pet Age Calculator?

The pet age calculator converts a dog or cat's age into human-equivalent years. Since aging speed varies by breed size, larger dogs are calculated separately from smaller ones.

How it's calculated

Dogs age roughly 15 human years in their first year and 24 by their second, adding about 4 to 5 years per year after that.

The 'multiply by 7' rule is wrong

If 1 dog-year equaled 7 human years, that would mean a 1-year-old dog is a 7-year-old human — which does not explain why dogs can reproduce at 1 year old while humans cannot at 7. Real aging is steep at first, then levels off. Larger breeds also age faster: small dogs often live past 15 years, while large breeds may only reach 8 to 10.

Good to know

  • Large breeds age faster partly because of their rapid growth rate — packing so much growth into a short window is linked to earlier onset of age-related conditions.
  • Cats show much less size-related variation in lifespan than dogs do, since cat breeds vary less in body size.
  • A 2020 study proposed a formula based on DNA methylation patterns (human age ≈ 16 × ln(dog age) + 31), showing just how extreme early-life aging is at the molecular level.
  • Dogs are typically classified as senior starting around age 7, prompting more frequent checkups — large breeds sometimes as early as 5 to 6.
  • Cats are notorious for hiding pain, so by the time symptoms are visible, a condition may already be advanced — one reason accurate age tracking matters for checkup scheduling.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Is multiplying dog years by 7 accurate?

Not really. Aging is much faster in the first two years and slows afterward, and it also varies by breed size.

Q. Why do larger dogs have shorter lifespans?

Rapid growth rate and the resulting cellular aging, along with higher rates of certain conditions, are commonly cited factors.

Q. At what age is a dog considered senior?

Small breeds are often considered senior around 7, while large breeds may reach that stage by 5 to 6.
