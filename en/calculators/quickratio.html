What is a Quick Percentage Calculator?

This calculator instantly answers questions like 'What percent of B is A?' or 'What is A percent of B?'

How it's calculated

A is what percent of B = A ÷ B × 100
A percent of B = B × A ÷ 100

Mental math shortcuts for percentages

Move the decimal point one place for 10%, halve that for 5%, move it two places for 1%. One especially useful trick: A% of B equals B% of A. '4% of 75' is hard to picture, but flip it to '75% of 4' and the answer (3) becomes obvious.

Good to know

  • Percent and percentage points aren't the same. A rise from 40% to 44% is both a 4-percentage-point increase and a 10% increase — which is why financial reporting always distinguishes 'pp' from '%.'
  • A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease doesn't return to the original. 100 → 150 → 75.
  • The percent sign (%) evolved from a handwritten abbreviation of the Italian 'per cento' (per hundred).
  • Per mille (‰) is a parts-per-thousand notation used for things like blood alcohol concentration.
  • Basis points (bp) in finance represent 0.01% — a '25bp increase' means a 0.25 percentage point rise.

Frequently asked questions

Q. What does a 100% increase mean?

Double the original. A 200% increase means triple, 300% means quadruple.

Q. How do I mentally calculate 4% of 75?

Flip it: 75% of 4 gives 3 immediately, since A% of B equals B% of A.

Q. When should I use % versus percentage points (pp)?

Use pp for the difference between two percentages; use % for relative change from an original value.
