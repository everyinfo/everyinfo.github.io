What is a Simple Average Calculator?

This calculator computes the arithmetic mean of a set of numbers you enter — a straightforward tool for learning the concept of average.

How it's calculated

Average = sum of all values ÷ count of values
For example, the average of 3, 5, and 7 is (3+5+7)÷3 = 5.

Good to know

  • The average is the most basic way to summarize a set of numbers into a single representative value — used everywhere from test scores to weather data.
  • If one value is much larger or smaller than the rest, the average can end up far from where most of the actual values are.
  • An average doesn't have to be a number that actually appears in the original data — a class average of 76.4 doesn't mean any student actually scored 76.4.
  • Despite its simplicity, calculating an average is the starting point for nearly all of statistics.
  • As the list of numbers grows, adding them by hand gets tedious — which is exactly when a calculator becomes genuinely useful.

Frequently asked questions

Q. How do I calculate an average?

Add up all the values, then divide by how many values there are.

Q. What if the average isn't a whole number?

It's fine to express it as a decimal, or round it depending on what you need.

Q. What's the average of just one number?

That number itself is the average.
