🧂 Saltwater Concentration Calculator

Find the concentration, salt amount, or resulting concentration after diluting saltwater, with step-by-step solutions.

What is a Salt Water Concentration Calculator?

The salt water concentration calculator computes the concentration percentage from the amounts of salt and water, or calculates how much salt or water is needed to reach a target concentration.

How it's calculated

Concentration (%) = solute (salt) ÷ solution (salt + water) × 100
The denominator is the total solution, not just the water.

The most common mistake

Dissolving 10g of salt in 100g of water does not give 10% concentration — it gives about 9.1%.

10 ÷ (10 + 100) × 100 = 9.09%

The confusion comes from using water (100) instead of the total solution (110) as the denominator. Remembering that the amount of salt itself never changes solves most concentration problems.

Good to know

  • Evaporating water leaves the salt amount unchanged but raises concentration. Adding water leaves salt unchanged but lowers concentration.
  • Average ocean salinity is about 3.5%, but it varies by region — under 1% in the Baltic Sea (heavy freshwater inflow) and over 4% in the Red Sea (high evaporation).
  • The Dead Sea is about 30% salt — so dense that a person floats effortlessly. Almost nothing can survive there, hence the name.
  • Human body fluid is about 0.9% salt. That is exactly why medical saline solution is mixed at that concentration — it keeps cells from shrinking or bursting.
  • Salt water freezes at a lower temperature and boils at a higher one than pure water — the same principle behind spreading road salt in winter and adding salt to pasta water.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Is 10g of salt in 100g of water 10% concentration?

No. It is about 9.1%. The denominator for concentration is the total solution (110g), not just the water.

Q. What happens if I add 100g of water to 200g of 10% salt water?

The salt amount stays at 20g, and the total becomes 300g, giving about 6.7%.

Q. Is there a limit to how much salt can dissolve?

Yes. At room temperature, water can dissolve up to about 36g of salt per 100g (saturation), for a maximum concentration of about 26%.
