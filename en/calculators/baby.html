👶 Baby Age Calculator

Track your baby's age in days, weeks, and months, with 100-day and first-birthday milestones.

What is a Baby Age Calculator?

This calculator computes a baby's exact age in days, weeks, and months from their birth date, useful for tracking vaccination schedules and introducing solid foods.

How it's calculated

The calculation converts the time elapsed since birth into days, weeks, and months. Month counts are typically based on the date recurring each month from the birth date.

Why baby age in months gets confusing

Unlike adult age, which typically increases once per year, infant development is tracked in months because growth happens so rapidly that yearly increments aren't precise enough. Vaccination schedules and developmental checkups are almost always specified in terms of 'months old,' not calendar dates.

Good to know

  • '4 weeks old' and '1 month old' aren't exactly the same — since months vary from 28 to 31 days, 'month old' typically refers to the date recurring, not a fixed 4-week interval.
  • Well-baby checkups and immunization schedules are built around tightly-specified age-in-months windows, so knowing the precise month count matters for not missing a recommended visit.
  • Growth percentile charts, used to track a baby's height and weight against population norms, also rely on accurate age-in-months as their reference point.
  • Introducing solid foods is commonly recommended starting around 4 to 6 months — again based on age in months, not a specific calendar date.
  • For babies born prematurely, 'corrected age' (calculated from the original due date rather than actual birth date) is often used instead when assessing developmental milestones.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Is a baby exactly 1 month old at 30 days?

Not necessarily. Month age is typically based on the date recurring from birth, and can be anywhere from 28 to 31 days depending on the month.

Q. What if we miss a vaccination window?

Most vaccines can still be given outside the standard schedule. Consult a pediatrician to arrange a catch-up plan.

Q. What is corrected age?

For premature babies, it's an age calculated from the original due date rather than the actual birth date, used to more fairly assess developmental progress relative to term babies.
